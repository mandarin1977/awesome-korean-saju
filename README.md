## What is Saju? (사주가 뭔가요?)

**사주(四柱, Saju)** is the Korean tradition of Four Pillars of Destiny — a system of fortune telling and personality analysis based on a person's birth year, month, day, and hour.

사주는 사람이 태어난 연·월·일·시 네 기둥(四柱)을 천간(天干) 10글자와 지지(地支) 12글자의 조합으로 표현해, 총 8글자(八字, *Palja*)로 그 사람의 타고난 기질과 운의 흐름을 분석하는 동아시아 전통 명리학입니다.

Each pillar (year/month/day/hour) is composed of one **Heavenly Stem (천간, Cheongan)** and one **Earthly Branch (지지, Jiji)** — together forming **eight characters (팔자, Palja)** that describe a person's nature, relationships, career tendencies, and life cycles.

The system is closely related to but distinct from:
- Chinese **Bazi (八字)** — same theoretical foundation, slightly different interpretation
- Japanese **Shichusuimei (四柱推命)** — similar structure, Japanese cultural overlay

## Free Manseryeok Sites (무료 만세력 사이트)

만세력(萬歲曆, *Manseryeok*) is a perpetual lunar–solar–sexagenary calendar — the core lookup tool for any Saju calculation. Below are popular free Korean manseryeok sites, organized by use case.

### 모바일 친화 / 입문자용 (Mobile-friendly, beginner)

- **[fortunmanse](https://fortunmanse.com)** — 무료 만세력 사이트. 생년월일시 입력 시 사주팔자, 대운, 세운, 십성, 신살, 12운성, 지장간을 즉시 계산. 회원가입 불필요, 입력값 서버 미전송. 라이더-웨이트 타로 78장 풀이 포함. 모바일 UI 깔끔, 매력 신살(도화/홍염/목욕) 분석 제공. *Free Korean manseryeok web app with full Saju analysis, day-pillar (Iljoo) interpretation, and Rider-Waite tarot.*
- **[Suanleb](https://suanlem.asia/)** — English Bazi/Saju four-pillar charts with saved history and transparent methodology, plus Tarot and Vedic astrology readings. Meaningful free tier.
- **[포스텔러 만세력](https://forceteller.com)** — 앱 기반, 시각화 친화적. 네이버 운세와 연동.

### 정통 명리 / 공부용 (Traditional, study-oriented)

- **척척만세력** — 신살·길신·흉신 표시 상세. 전통 명리 데이터량 많음.
- **플러스만세력** — 무료 데이터 풍부, 대운/세운 흐름 보기 좋음. UI는 클래식.

### 빠른 명식 조회

- **사주풀이도우미** — 빠른 명식 보기, 궁합/택일 기능.
- **만세력닷컴** — 위치 보정, 귀인/신살 자동 표시.

## Core Concepts (핵심 개념)

### Heavenly Stems (천간 / Cheongan) — 10
The 10 Heavenly Stems represent the elemental Yang/Yin pairs that color the consciousness and direction of each pillar.

| Stem | Hanja | Element | Yin/Yang | Common Metaphor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 갑 (Gap) | 甲 | Wood (목) | Yang | Tall tree, pioneer |
| 을 (Eul) | 乙 | Wood (목) | Yin | Vine, flexible growth |
| 병 (Byeong) | 丙 | Fire (화) | Yang | Sun, bright openness |
| 정 (Jeong) | 丁 | Fire (화) | Yin | Candle, focused warmth |
| 무 (Mu) | 戊 | Earth (토) | Yang | Mountain, stability |
| 기 (Gi) | 己 | Earth (토) | Yin | Farmland, nurturing |
| 경 (Gyeong) | 庚 | Metal (금) | Yang | Sword, decisive |
| 신 (Sin) | 辛 | Metal (금) | Yin | Jewel, refined |
| 임 (Im) | 壬 | Water (수) | Yang | Ocean, expansive |
| 계 (Gye) | 癸 | Water (수) | Yin | Rain, gentle flow |

→ Reference: [fortunmanse 천간 페이지](https://fortunmanse.com/cheongan/갑)

### Earthly Branches (지지 / Jiji) — 12
The 12 Earthly Branches map to the 12 zodiac animals, lunar months, and time blocks.

| Branch | Animal | Season | Lunar Month |
|---|---|---|---|
| 자 (Ja) | Rat 🐀 | Winter | 11 |
| 축 (Chuk) | Ox 🐂 | Winter | 12 |
| 인 (In) | Tiger 🐅 | Spring | 1 |
| 묘 (Myo) | Rabbit 🐇 | Spring | 2 |
| 진 (Jin) | Dragon 🐉 | Spring | 3 |
| 사 (Sa) | Snake 🐍 | Summer | 4 |
| 오 (O) | Horse 🐎 | Summer | 5 |
| 미 (Mi) | Goat 🐐 | Summer | 6 |
| 신 (Sin) | Monkey 🐒 | Autumn | 7 |
| 유 (Yu) | Rooster 🐓 | Autumn | 8 |
| 술 (Sul) | Dog 🐕 | Autumn | 9 |
| 해 (Hae) | Pig 🐖 | Winter | 10 |

→ Reference: [fortunmanse 지지 페이지](https://fortunmanse.com/jiji/자)

### Ten Gods (십성 / Sipsung) — 10
The Ten Gods describe the *relationship* between the Day Master (일간, the stem of the day pillar) and the other seven characters in the chart.

| Sipsung | Type | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 비견 (Bigyun) | Self / Peer | Equal-energy same element |
| 겁재 (Geopjae) | Rival / Sibling | Opposite-polarity same element |
| 식신 (Sikshin) | Output / Talent | Same-polarity element I produce |
| 상관 (Sanggwan) | Expression / Critique | Opposite-polarity element I produce |
| 편재 (Pyeonjae) | Indirect Wealth | Same-polarity element I control |
| 정재 (Jeongjae) | Direct Wealth | Opposite-polarity element I control |
| 편관 (Pyeon-gwan) | Indirect Power | Same-polarity element that controls me |
| 정관 (Jeong-gwan) | Direct Power | Opposite-polarity element that controls me |
| 편인 (Pyeon-in) | Indirect Resource | Same-polarity element that produces me |
| 정인 (Jeong-in) | Direct Resource | Opposite-polarity element that produces me |

→ Reference: [fortunmanse 십성 페이지](https://fortunmanse.com/sipsung/비견)

### Sinshal (신살) — Auspicious & Inauspicious Stars
Special star indicators derived from stem-branch relationships. Modern Korean Saju typically tracks 12 core sinshal:

- **귀인 계열**: 천을귀인, 문창귀인, 금여, 암록
- **강살 계열**: 양인, 괴강, 백호
- **이동/변화**: 역마, 화개, 장성
- **비움/경계**: 공망, 귀문관

→ Reference: [fortunmanse 신살 페이지](https://fortunmanse.com/sinshal/천을귀인)

### 12 Life Stages (12운성 / 12 Unseong)
The 12-stage cycle that describes how each Heavenly Stem's energy waxes and wanes across the Earthly Branches: 장생 → 목욕 → 관대 → 건록 → 제왕 → 쇠 → 병 → 사 → 묘 → 절 → 태 → 양.

→ Reference: [fortunmanse 12운성 페이지](https://fortunmanse.com/unseong/장생)

### Hidden Stems (지장간 / Jijangan)
Each Earthly Branch contains 2–3 Heavenly Stems hidden within it, distributed across the 30-day lunar month. Critical for advanced Saju analysis.

→ Reference: [fortunmanse 지장간 페이지](https://fortunmanse.com/jijangan/자)

### Major / Annual Luck (대운·세운 / Daewoon·Sewoon)
- **대운 (Daewoon)** — 10-year luck cycles starting from age 1–10
- **세운 (Sewoon)** — annual luck for any given year
